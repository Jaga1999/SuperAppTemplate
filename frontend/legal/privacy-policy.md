## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you can ask to access, correct, delete or export
your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent. Many of these are self-service in **Settings**.
For anything else, write to **[privacy@example.com]**. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

## 8. Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text; passkeys and two-step verification are available; sessions can be ended
on every device instantly; data is encrypted in transit.

## 9. Children

SuperApp is not intended for children under 16 (or the age of digital consent where you live).
